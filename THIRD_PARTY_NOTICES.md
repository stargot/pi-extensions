## pi-config

Two extensions derive from work by Eero Alvar
([amosblomqvist](https://github.com/amosblomqvist)) in
[amosblomqvist/pi-config](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/pi-config):

- `extensions/prompt-snippets` — ported from its `prompt-snippets` extension
  without changes to the logic.
- `extensions/web` — started from parts of its `web-search` extension and has
  since been largely rewritten.

The upstream repository does not declare a license; attribution is kept here
and in the READMEs of those extensions.
